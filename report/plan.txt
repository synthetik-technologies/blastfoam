Report planning notes
---------------

Concepts to be included in the report, in no particular order...

Dispersed Multiphase Flow:
 o Comparison Lagrangian vs Eulerian vs MP-PIC vs Population Balance Modelling
 o Dispersed phase granular temperature modelling - necessary for this context? - modelling approaches
 o Compressible vs incompressible solid phase effects
 o Potential for implementing MUSIC or QMOM solvers in blastfoam?
 
Aluminium Particle Burning:
 o Surface heating and ignition modelling
 o Surface burn rate, pressure dependency, Al oxide formation effects, dependence on surrounding composition
 o Connection to the ideal detonation solver - particle heating, ignition during early phases (pre-CJ, early isentropic expansion)

Initiation:
 o Initialised from output of ideal detonation solver: detonation speed, energy release, JWLC equation of state parameters, CJ composition and freeze-out temperature composition
 o Ideal solver currently assumes inert Al on isentrope and thermal equilibrium.
 o Programmed burn at ideal detonation speed, JWLC equation of state for detonation products
 o Potential to update 'ideal' solver to incorporate Al heating (and burning?) along isentrope to provide better initial condition for simulation? Data along isentrope is available
 o Potential to use full equation of state (BKW/exp-6) in blastFoam to avoid two-stage initiation. 

Numerical Multiphase Solver:
 o Document and assess existing compressible multiphase solver in blastfoam
 o Compare with approach in gunIB1D ~/Codes/gunIB1D on range of 1D test cases (Houim / Lowe) to determine whether blastfoam solver need improvment.
 o Construct simple test cases for solver applied to 3 phases (JWL/gas/Al) to determine accuracy / find problems

Afterburning Reaction Kinetics:
 o Suitable chemical kinetic mechanism for reaction of combination of products, aluminium and air
 o Importance of soot formation / aluminium oxide condensation?
 
Literature Review / Articles to Consider: in report/external_sources


Test cases to incorporate
-------------------------

- 0D
 o Phase transfer from JWL to reacting multicomponent gas
 o Phase transfer from Al to reacting multicomponent gas
 o Constant pressure / constant volume aluminium combustion
 o High temperature air thermodynamics (dissociations, high temperature Cp)
 o Aluminium particle surface heating and ignition
 
- 1D
 o Shock wave in air comparable to blast, showing dissociation behind shock
 o Simple 1D multiphase problems (interface advection, Riemann problem, shock bubble)
 o Verification of planar ZND detonation in blastfoam, comparison to predicted profile from EuDetCode
 o 1D spherically symmetric detonation. Two phase (JWL/gas) and three-phase (JWL/Al/gas).
 o Investigation of effect of various chosen transfer conditions for phase transfer (JwL->gas) on results.
 o Potentially 1D spherical detonation test with BKW / exp-6 phase for both products and air and switch from instant equilbirium above specified temperature / density to finite-rate chemistry at lower temperatures / densities?

- 2D / 3D
 o Multi-dimensional repeat of 1D spherical tests
 o 2D axisymmetric cylindrical charge equivalents of 1D spherically symmetric tests. 
 o Refined study of mixing/combustion at front between detonation products and air (and front between Al and air).
 o Adaptive mesh refinement assessment.
 o Comparison of Lagrangian particle / Eulerian
